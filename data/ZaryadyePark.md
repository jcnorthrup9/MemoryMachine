# SITE PRECEDENT: ZARYADYE PARK
**Location:** Moscow, Russia  
**Architect:** Diller Scofidio + Renfro  
**Concept:** Wild Urbanism  

---

## 1. GEOMETRIC LOGIC: HYBRID LANDSCAPE
- **Topography:** Undulating mounds and terraces that seamlessly blend hardscape and softscape.
- **Microclimates:** Terraced landscapes create simulated natural biomes (tundra, steppe, forest, and wetland).
- **The Overlook:** A dramatic, V-shaped floating bridge cantilevering out over the Moskva River.

## 2. PROGRAMMATIC ZONES
| Zone ID | Label | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SOFT_01** | Wild Tundra | Natural, untamed planting wrapping over and concealing architectural forms. |
| **HARD_01** | Dissolving Paving | Hexagonal stone paving that gradually breaks apart and fades into the soil. |
| **PROG_01** | Sunken Pavilions | Subterranean program (cafes, galleries) concealed beneath landscape mounds. |

## 3. SPATIAL RELATIONSHIPS
1. **The Gradient Rule:** There must be no hard, distinct boundaries between paving and planting; materials must dissolve and pixelate into one another.
2. **The Topographic Rule:** Architecture must act as landscape. Roofs of `PROG_01` interventions should function as walkable hills planted with `SOFT_01`.
3. **The Cantilever Rule:** Major viewpoints or structures should aggressively stretch out over empty space or water boundaries.
