## 4. O. T. JOHNSON BUILDING
**Location:** Downtown Los Angeles, CA  
**Architect:** John Parkinson  
**Concept:** Romanesque Revival Commercial  

### A. Spatial & Geometric Logic
- **Structure:** Constructed in 1902 as a 7-story office structure, later reduced to 2 stories. Steel-frame design with brick cladding.
- **Facade:** Glazed pressed-brick facade, iron-and-glass ground floor elements, and paired/fluted columns at the main entrance.
- **History:** A 2007 fire burned away mid-century modernization (shingle cladding), exposing long-hidden Romanesque features like large arched windows.

### B. Visitor Experience & Harvested Sentiment
- *Observation:* The building serves as a literal architectural palimpsest, where destruction (fire) led to the rediscovery of its original 1902 aesthetic, mirroring the Memory Machine's themes of decay, erasure, and uncovering latent data.
