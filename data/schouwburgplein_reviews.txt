Theatre Square (or in Dutch : Schouwburgplein) is a plaza situated in the heart of the city of Rotterdam, and is flanked by the municipal theatre, concert hall, restaurants, and cafes. An urban stage and an interactive open space, the 12.250 square meter square is designed by West 8, the landscape architecture firm founded by Adriaan Geuze.

Since its redesign and opening in 1996, Schouwburgplein has evolved into a vibrant multifunctional public space in Rotterdam, hosting a wide array of events and activities that integrate it into the city's cultural calendar.
