# SITE PRECEDENT: GARDENS BY THE BAY
**Location:** Singapore  
**Architect:** Grant Associates / WilkinsonEyre  
**Concept:** City in a Garden / Biomimicry  

---

## 1. GEOMETRIC LOGIC: BIOMIMETIC FORMS
- **Supertrees:** Towering vertical gardens (25-50m tall) that act as environmental engines, rainwater collectors, and shading devices.
- **Conservatories:** Massive glass domes with biomorphic, shell-like ribbed structures spanning without interior columns.
- **Organic Flows:** Winding, leaf-vein inspired pathways that navigate through incredibly dense, lush tropical flora.

## 2. PROGRAMMATIC ZONES
| Zone ID | Label | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PROG_01** | Supertree Grove | Vertical trellises covered in epiphytes, ferns, and programmable lighting. |
| **SOFT_01** | Tropical Gardens | High-density, lush planting with a focus on massive biodiversity and color. |
| **BLUE_01** | Dragonfly Lake | Large ecological water bodies designed for natural cooling and runoff filtration. |

## 3. SPATIAL RELATIONSHIPS
1. **The Verticality Rule:** `SOFT_01` (Softscape) must stretch aggressively upwards via artificial structures, breaking the 2D plane.
2. **The Skyway Rule:** Elevated pedestrian paths must connect major vertical elements, allowing circulation above the tree canopy.
3. **The Biomimicry Rule:** All built architectural forms must derive their structural and aesthetic logic from nature (e.g., orchids, ribs, webs).
