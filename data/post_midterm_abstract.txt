A computational design project exploring the intersection of human memory decay, artificial intelligence, and procedural architecture.

Post-midterm development introduces the Autonomous Spatial Harvester—a custom-built web-scraping and computer vision pipeline that extracts qualitative human sentiment and quantitative spatial data directly from the internet. This framework was tested through three forensic case studies: the sensory interior of Bottega Louie, the structural facade of the O.T. Johnson Building, and the parametric decay of the Nakagin Capsule Tower.

These automated extraction techniques are now being deployed onto Pershing Square, an ultimate urban palimpsest, to generate a hallucinated masterplan born entirely from synthesized public memory and digital erasure.
