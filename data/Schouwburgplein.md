# SITE PRECEDENT: SCHOUWBURGPLEIN
**Location:** Rotterdam, Netherlands  
**Architect:** West 8  
**Concept:** The Urban Stage  

---

## 1. GEOMETRIC LOGIC: THE PLATFORM
- **The Stage:** A raised, lightweight deck elevated above the city level, clearing the underground parking roof beneath it.
- **Kinetic Elements:** Four massive, red, hydraulic lighting masts inspired by harbor shipping cranes.
- **Material Zones:** Distinct rectangular patches of wood, metal, and epoxy to define varying textures and uses without walls.

## 2. PROGRAMMATIC ZONES
| Zone ID | Label | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **HARD_01** | The Deck | Raised lightweight platform utilizing industrial materials (timber, steel panels). |
| **PROG_01** | Kinetic Masts | Giant red movable lighting cranes that citizens can manually control. |
| **SOFT_01** | Sunken Gardens | Sparse, highly contained rectangular vegetation cut directly into the deck. |

## 3. SPATIAL RELATIONSHIPS
1. **The Elevation Rule:** The primary ground plane must be lifted by 35cm, disconnecting it from the surrounding city streets and acting as a stage.
2. **The Industrial Rule:** Materials and interventions should reflect a raw, port-city aesthetic rather than natural organic forms.
3. **The Void Rule:** The center of the site must remain overwhelmingly empty to allow for flexible public gatherings and festivals.
