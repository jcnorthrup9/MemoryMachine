# SITE PRECEDENT: PARC DE LA VILLETTE
**Location:** Paris, France  
**Architect:** Bernard Tschumi  
**Concept:** Point, Line, Surface / Deconstructivism  

---

## 1. GEOMETRIC LOGIC: THE GRID
- **The Follies (Points):** Bright red, deconstructivist architectural structures arranged on a strict 120m x 120m grid. They serve as anchors for activities and orientation.
- **The Lines:** High-speed pedestrian movement axes (galleries) and meandering, organic paths (cinematic promenades).
- **The Surfaces:** Large, open geometric lawns used for unprogrammed recreation.

## 2. PROGRAMMATIC ZONES
| Zone ID | Label | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PROG_01** | The Folly | Rigid, red architectural interventions strictly bound to the grid intersections. |
| **SOFT_01** | Open Lawn | Expansive geometric grass surfaces bounded by harsh linear paths. |
| **HARD_01** | The Promenade | Covered walkways, steel bridges, and paved axes intersecting the park. |

## 3. SPATIAL RELATIONSHIPS
1. **The Grid Rule:** All `PROG_01` (Follies) must be placed at strict, regular intervals, ignoring the natural topography.
2. **The Disconnect Rule:** Architecture and landscape should intentionally collide rather than blend seamlessly.
3. **The Cinematic Path:** Pedestrian paths should weave organically through the strict grid, creating moments of spontaneous discovery.
