[TARGET NAME] (Russian: Парк Зарядье) is a landscape urban park located adjacent to Red Square in Moscow, Russia, on the site of the former Zaryadye district.

The park was inaugurated on 9 September 2017 by Russian president Vladimir Putin and Moscow Mayor Sergey Sobyanin.

Zaryadye (Russian: Зарядье, IPA: [zɐˈrʲædʲje]) is a historical district in Moscow established in the 12th or 13th century within Kitai-gorod, between Varvarka Street and the Moskva River.

The park opened in November 2017.
