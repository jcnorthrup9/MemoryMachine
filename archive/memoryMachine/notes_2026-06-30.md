## 01 // Background — Speaking Notes

### Architectural Palimpsest / Public Memory

The city as unstable archive. Architectural witness marks, strata of successive interventions, blocked windows become structural. The SCI-Arc building. The Bradbury. The 110 freeway stairways to nowhere. Memory as spatial constraint.

Neither total erasure nor rigid preservation correctly interprets memory. Both try to stabilize what cannot be stabilized. In practice, memory always leaks.

### Noise is Not Nothing

Information persists in static, in drone, in white noise. The apophenic tendency: to mistake noise for meaning, to find pattern in randomness. Architecture and neural networks share this programming — forever hallucinating, constructing coherence from what is missing.
