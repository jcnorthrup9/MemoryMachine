DATA HARVESTING

The Personal Archive

The first stage involves the extraction of digital remnants. Every residence leaves a trail: low-resolution photographs, scanned documents, and fragmented sensory descriptions. These are not just data; they are the anchors for reconstruction.

POINT CLOUD DECAY

Entropy Vectors

As the machine processes the harvest, it introduces translational entropy. Points that cannot be verified by sensory data are allowed to "drift," creating the spatial voids characteristic of the Palimpsest.
